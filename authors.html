Dr. Sarah Chen

Neuroscientist & Contemplative Scholar

Dr. Sarah Chen bridges the worlds of neuroscience research and contemplative practice, exploring how modern brain science illuminates ancient wisdom about consciousness and human potential. Her work exemplifies the kind of rigorous yet spiritually informed inquiry that Theosis champions.

With a PhD in Cognitive Neuroscience from Stanford and fifteen years of meditation practice, Dr. Chen brings both scientific precision and contemplative depth to questions about the nature of mind and awareness.

Write for Theosis

We're always seeking quality writing that embodies our mission: serious spirituality for thoughtful people. We publish authors who explore consciousness, wisdom, and human potential with both intellectual rigor and practical insight.

What We Look For

  • Intellectual Rigor: Well-researched, thoughtfully argued perspectives that engage seriously with complex questions
  • Practical Wisdom: Insights that translate into meaningful guidance for everyday life
  • Authentic Voice: Personal experience integrated with broader understanding
  • Bridging Perspectives: Work that connects scientific understanding with spiritual insight
  • Original Thinking: Fresh perspectives that move beyond familiar categories

Topics We Explore

  • Consciousness studies and philosophy of mind
  • AI, technology, and human consciousness
  • Post-traditional spirituality and wisdom practices
  • Science and spirituality intersections
  • Practical applications of contemplative insights
  • Cultural and philosophical critiques of materialism

Submission Process

Send us a brief pitch (200-300 words) outlining your proposed article or book project. Include:

  • Your central thesis or question
  • Why it matters to our audience
  • Your background and qualifications
  • Any relevant previous work

Note: We value quality over quantity. We're looking for authors who share our commitment to depth, authenticity, and intellectual honesty.

Submit Your Pitch

Email us your proposal and we'll respond within two weeks.

Response Time

We review all submissions carefully and respond within 2-3 weeks. Due to volume, we can only respond to submissions that fit our editorial focus.

Editorial Standards

All accepted pieces go through collaborative editing to ensure clarity and consistency with our editorial voice while preserving your unique perspective.
